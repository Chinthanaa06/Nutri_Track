# Literature survey

1. **WHO Child Growth Standards (2006).** WHO Multicentre Growth Reference Study Group. *WHO Child Growth Standards: Length/height-for-age, weight-for-age, weight-for-length, weight-for-height and body mass index-for-age: Methods and development.* Foundation for standardized child growth assessment.
2. **WHO (2009).** *WHO Child Growth Standards: Growth velocity based on weight, length and head circumference.* Provides context for longitudinal growth monitoring.
3. **WHO (2013).** *Guideline: Updates on the management of severe acute malnutrition in infants and children.* Supports the need for appropriate referral and clinical management.
4. **UNICEF, WHO and World Bank (2024).** *Joint Child Malnutrition Estimates.* Global and regional estimates and definitions for stunting, wasting and underweight.
5. **Lundberg, S. M. and Lee, S.-I. (2017).** “A Unified Approach to Interpreting Model Predictions.” *Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems 30.* Introduces SHAP.
6. **Sculley et al. (2015).** “Hidden Technical Debt in Machine Learning Systems.” *NeurIPS.* Discusses operational complexity in ML systems.
7. **Humble, J. and Farley, D. (2010).** *Continuous Delivery.* Addison-Wesley. Background for automated build, test and delivery practices.
8. **FastAPI documentation.** Official framework documentation for typed API design and automatic OpenAPI documentation.
