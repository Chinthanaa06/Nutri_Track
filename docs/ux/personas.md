# User personas

## Persona 1 — Community health worker
Needs quick, low-friction data entry on a shared device, clear validation and a simple follow-up cue. Limited time and variable connectivity are key constraints.

## Persona 2 — School nurse
Needs to record periodic measurements, compare visits and understand why a record is flagged. Prefers readable charts and exportable records.

## Persona 3 — Programme supervisor
Needs an aggregate view of screening categories and a prioritized follow-up list. Needs clear caveats about data quality and screening-only use.
